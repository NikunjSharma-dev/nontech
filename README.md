## 📊 Summary of Individual Case Studies

### 1. 🌿 Supply Chain Carbon Emission Optimization
- **Focus:** Multi-modal freight optimization, Scope 1–3 GHG accounting, and vehicle miles traveled (VMT) minimization (~28% emission intensity reduction).
- **Repo:** [`NikunjSharma-dev/supply-chain-carbon-optimization`](https://github.com/NikunjSharma-dev/supply-chain-carbon-optimization)

### 2. 💧 Water Wise Sustainability Strategy (IIT Guwahati)
- **Focus:** 5.5 MLD campus consumption audit, pipeline distribution infrastructure fix for MSH & Gaurang hostels, IoT leak detection grid.
- **Repo:** [`NikunjSharma-dev/water-wise-sustainability-strategy`](https://github.com/NikunjSharma-dev/water-wise-sustainability-strategy)

### 3. 🚀 The Product Perspective (INDmoney Strategy)
- **Focus:** Wealthtech competitive teardown (92.09M client benchmark vs Groww/Zerodha), user personas (Dev Mishra/Harshita Shetty), RICE feature scoring.
- **Repo:** [`NikunjSharma-dev/product-perspective-deck`](https://github.com/NikunjSharma-dev/product-perspective-deck)

### 4. ❄️ Albatross Energetics (IIT Bombay)
- **Focus:** Low-GWP refrigerant selection ($GWP < 500$, R-1234yf blends), evaporator/condenser heat exchanger sizing, dynamic control logic, and cost analysis.
- **Repo:** [`NikunjSharma-dev/albatross-business-proposal`](https://github.com/NikunjSharma-dev/albatross-business-proposal)

### 5. 📊 Executive Business Presentation Framework
- **Focus:** Sustainable fashion global supply chain overhaul, polyester environmental accounting (57M tons), and modular C-suite slide design.
- **Repo:** [`NikunjSharma-dev/executive-deck-framework`](https://github.com/NikunjSharma-dev/executive-deck-framework)

### 6. 🚜 Organigo Agritech Startup
- **Focus:** Farm-to-table micro-fulfillment, post-harvest wastage reduction (35% down to < 8%), farmer fair pricing, D2C subscription unit economics.
- **Repo:** [`NikunjSharma-dev/Organigo`](https://github.com/NikunjSharma-dev/Organigo)

### 7. ⚡ EcoEdge Renewable Energy Valuation
- **Focus:** Turnaround strategy for renewable energy assets, integrated Excel financial modeling (`Eco_Edge_final.xlsx`) covering DCF, LCOE, and DSCR.
- **Repo:** [`NikunjSharma-dev/ECO-EDGE`](https://github.com/NikunjSharma-dev/ECO-EDGE)
