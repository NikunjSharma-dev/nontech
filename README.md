## 📊 Strategic Case Study Summaries

### 1. 🌿 Supply Chain Carbon Emission Optimization (InternIIT)
- **Problem:** Scope 1, 2, and 3 GHG emissions in industrial logistics.
- **Solution:** Developed an optimization framework identifying modal shift opportunities (road-to-rail conversion), warehouse route clustering, and fleet electrification to reduce carbon footprint by ~28%.
- **Standalone Repo:** [NikunjSharma-dev/supply-chain-carbon-optimization](https://github.com/NikunjSharma-dev/supply-chain-carbon-optimization)

### 2. 💧 Water Wise Sustainability Strategy (Kriti Challenge)
- **Problem:** Municipal Non-Revenue Water (NRW) loss and industrial effluent management.
- **Solution:** Designed smart metering infrastructure recommendations, IoT leak detection strategy, and circular wastewater recycling financial models.
- **Standalone Repo:** [NikunjSharma-dev/water-wise-sustainability-strategy](https://github.com/NikunjSharma-dev/water-wise-sustainability-strategy)
